# Divergences

> Parent: [[Folksprak]] · Import rules: [[Folksprak/Word formation guidelines]]

Folksprak exists online in two older bodies of material: the folksprak.org draft (with its wiki and Munin dictionary) and Parke's English–Folksprak dictionaries (2010–2011). The Folksprak in this vault is a fork that re-derives the language from Proto-Germanic. This page records where the two differ, for anyone arriving from the online documentation.

Note on upstream status: in the draft, only phonology/orthography, the personal pronouns, basic conjugation, declension, and sentence structure carry "draft" status. Its correlatives, adpositions, word functions, modal verbs, timeforms, negation, and plural are all marked "proposal only" upstream — a divergence there diverges from a proposal, not from a norm.

## Phonology

- The vault adds **/x/** (Latin x, ᛉ). The draft considered an x-like sound and excluded it.
- The vault adds **/ʃ/** (Latin c, ᚳ). The draft has no /ʃ/.

## Orthography

- Front rounded vowels: vault **ø, y**; draft **ö ~ oy, ü ~ uy**.
- Runic mappings differ (runes are decorative in both systems):

| Sound | Vault | Draft |
| --- | --- | --- |
| /a/ | ᚪ | ᚨ |
| /o/ | ᚩ | ᛟ |
| /ø/ | ᛟ | ᛟᛇ |
| /y/ | ᚣ | ᚢᛇ |
| /k/ | ᛣ | ᚲ |
| /s/ | ᛋ | ᛊ |
| /ʃ/ | ᚳ | — |
| /x/ | ᛉ | — |

## Pronouns

Vault pronouns decline for case (genitive, possessive, accusative); the draft has nominative/accusative forms plus periphrasis with *to* and *av*.

| Gloss | Vault | Draft |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | ikk | ek ~ i |
| thou | du | du *(same)* |
| he | hi | han |
| she | si | hun |
| it | itt | hit |
| we | vi | vi *(same)* |
| you (pl.) | ji | je |
| they | dei | di |
| reflexive, acc. | sig | sek |
| reflexive, gen. | sin | periphrasis with egen |

The draft's gender-neutral 3rd singular *hen* has no vault counterpart.

## Grammar

- Noun plural: vault **-en**; draft proposal **-er**.
- Verb endings: vault past **-de**, active participle **-ende**, passive participle **-t**; draft -(e)de, -end, -(e)t.
- Auxiliaries: vault future **ville** + infinitive, perfect **have** + participle; draft skal/vil and hav.
- Modal set: vault durfe (permission), kunne (ability), mage (possibility), møte (necessity), skulle (obligation); draft proposal mute, kane, vile, skale, vöre, with no permission modal.
- **vare** 'to be' is the only irregular verb: present *ar*, imperative/preterite *var*. The draft does not specify an irregular verb.
- Word formation: the vault re-evolves words from Proto-Germanic; the draft selects compromise forms among the modern languages. The draft's derivational affixes (far-, -ar, -ing, -rid, -ber, -isk, -hed, un-) are unadopted here and are taken case by case.

Upstream corroboration: du, vi (we), har, dar, ve, vat, var, hur (how), tve (two), lang (long), kurt (short), grot (big), sver (heavy), kvin (woman), ver (adult male), barn (child), fader (father), fogel (bird), hund (dog), hud (skin), vurt (root), flesk (meat), ete (eat), bite, seje (see), denke (think), vete and kene (the know split), leve (live), slaje (hit), kome (come), lige (lie down), stae (stand), fale (fall), druke (push), sege (say), singe (sing), spile (play), vaske (wash), seks (six), ten (ten), elv (eleven), tvelv (twelve) match the draft/Munin forms; døe (die) and flyge (fly) match modulo vault respelling (döe, flüge) (several appear upstream mainly as correlative roots, e.g. *vilk sted, var* 'where'). **hur** was adopted from upstream in place of the coinage *vi*, specifically to avoid a homograph with vi 'we'; it keeps the h where the other \*hw- words drop it (ve, vat, var, vann) and stands as an accepted exception. Munin has no entries for many, few, other, kill, hunt, split, stab, scratch, dig, swim, walk, or sit.

The remaining vault entries (tve, lus, bryst, fruxt, ficc, durfe, kunigdøm, kaffie) have no counterpart in draft.html.
