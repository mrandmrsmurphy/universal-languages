| Gloss | at, by (location); at, on, in (time) |

| Cognates | English *by*, Dutch *bij*, Luxembourgish *bei*, German *bei* |
| Swadesh | [[Folksprak/Swadesh]] № 201 |

*Bi* marks a location or proximity to a landmark, as in “at the station” or “by the door.” It is also the default preposition for clock times, days and longer periods, as in “at noon,” “on Wednesday” and “in July.” Use [[Folksprak/Lexicon/ᛁᚾ|in]] for spatial containment. Express deadlines explicitly with “no later than.”

[Parke’s dictionary](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0Bx0T9Dxrods1dVVqaXVyOVZmSTg/view) gives *bi* under “by.” [Germanic etymology](https://ewa.saw-leipzig.de/articles/12960/en)
