[Parke’s Wordbok](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0Bx0T9Dxrods1em9yRGh6d1BacEU/view) gives *stoev* “dust, grit, particles.” The Folksprak form matches Danish and Norwegian *støv*. Norwegian traces its form to Low German *stof*.
