[Parke’s dictionary](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0Bx0T9Dxrods1dVVqaXVyOVZmSTg/view) gives *fordat*. Dutch *omdat* and Nordic *fordi* are structurally parallel. Nynorsk *fordi* continues Old Norse *fyrir því at*, roughly “for that [fact] that.” Nynorsk *av di* and Icelandic *af því að* use the parallel “from/of that” pattern. German *weil* developed from “while,” and English *because* from a phrase meaning “by reason of.”
