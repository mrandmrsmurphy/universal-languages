Adopted from the upstream (draft/Munin) form *hur*, replacing the earlier coinage *vi*, to avoid a homograph with [[Folksprak/Lexicon/ᚹᛁ|vi]] 'we'. Note this keeps the h where the other \*hw- words drop it (ve, vat, var, vann) — an accepted exception.
