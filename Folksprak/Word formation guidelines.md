# Word formation guidelines

> Parent: [[Folksprak]] · Upstream differences: [[Folksprak/Divergences]] · Lexicon: [[Folksprak/Lexicon/Lexicon]]

## Source principle

Folksprak vocabulary is **re-evolved from Proto-Germanic** by regular sound correspondences (see [[Folksprak/Anthropology#Prolegomena]]), not copied from any one modern Germanic language. Latin/Romance loans are not taken where a Germanic root exists (so spy 'vomit', never *vomite*). A word that cannot be derived this way is a loanword and gets an Etymology row in its lexicon entry instead of a Proto-Germanic row (e.g. [[Folksprak/Lexicon/ᛣᚪᚠᚠᛁᛖ|kaffie]]).

(The English-style palatalization; German sch and Scandinavian skj-/sj- show the same sound from the same source.)

## Shape rules

- A short stressed monosyllable doubles its final consonant: \*ik → [[Folksprak/Lexicon/ᛁᛣᛣ|ikk]], itt, mann.
- Vowel length is predictable (stressed + at most one following consonant = long) and is never written. See [[Folksprak/Phonology]].
- Stress falls on the first syllable and does not move with prefixes; particles are proclitic.

## Affixes

Inflectional (details in [[Folksprak/Morphophonology]] and [[Folksprak/Morphosyntax]]): plural **-en**, genitive **-s**, comparative **-er**, superlative **-est**, ordinal **-de**, adverb **-lik**; verbs: infinitive **-e**, past **-de**, active participle **-ende**, passive participle **-t**.

Derivational, attested: **-ig** (kunig 'kingly'), **-døm** (kunigdøm 'kingdom').

The upstream draft's word-function affixes (far-, -ar, -ing, -rid, -ber, -isk, -hed, un-, …) are proposal material even upstream and are adopted only case by case.

## Root reservations

Where two families compete, both may be kept with distinct senses, and some roots are reserved for a semantic field:

- The **flug-** family (German *Flügel*, Dutch *vleugel*) is reserved for flight vocabulary (fly, flying); the body part is [[Folksprak/Lexicon/ᚹᛁᚾᚷ|ving]] 'wing'.
- Attested sense splits: vid/bred (wide/broad), skog/vald (shaggy forest/wild wilderness), buk/mage (belly/stomach).

## Importing from upstream sources

The folksprak.org draft, the Munin dictionary, and Parke's dictionaries all use upstream conventions. Never import a form verbatim:

1. Respell the vowels: **ü, uy → y**; **ö, oy → ø**.
2. Check against vault phonology: upstream has no /x/ or /ʃ/ and avoided words that needed them, so vault forms may legitimately differ ([[Folksprak/Lexicon/ᚠᚱᚢᛉᛏ|fruxt]], [[Folksprak/Lexicon/ᚠᛁᚳᚳ|ficc]]).
3. Check [[Folksprak/Divergences]]: where the vault has already settled a different form (geve, not giv; dyr, not dir), the vault form wins.
4. Derive the IPA from [[Folksprak/Phonology]] and the runes from [[Folksprak/Orthography]]; mark derived values as derived in the entry.
