# Morphosyntax

> Parent: [[Folksprak]]

1. The subject may not be separated from the finite verb by any other word.
2. The ordinary position for the verb in a declarative sentence is as **second element**; in imperatives or questions, as the **first** element.
3. The grammatical subject must always come before any objects.

## Verbs

- Verbs inflect for **tense**, not number or person.
- Lexical form = infinitive (**+e**)
- Infinitive: **+ᛖ** / **+e**
- Present and imperative: bare form (−e)
- Past: **+ᛞᛖ** / **+de**
- Active participle: **+ᛖᚾᛞᛖ** / **+ende**
- Passive participle: **+ᛏ** / **+t**

### *vare* (to be) — only irregular verb

| Form | Runic | Latin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitive | ᚹᚪᚱᛖ | **vare** |
| Present | ᚪᚱ | **ar** |
| Imperative / preterite | ᚹᚪᚱ | **var** |

### Auxiliaries

Normally with infinitive; sometimes with past participle.

| | Runic | Latin | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Future | ᚹᛁᛚᛚᛖ | **ville** + inf. | will |
| Perfect | ᚻᚪᚹᛖ | **have** + PP | have |
| Passive | ᚹᚪᚱᛖ | **vare** + PP | be (passive) |
| Permission | ᛞᚢᚱᚠᛖ | **durfe** | be allowed |
| Ability | ᛣᚢᚾᚾᛖ | **kunne** | can |
| Possibility | ᛗᚪᚷᛖ | **mage** | might, may |
| Necessity | ᛗᛟᛏᛖ | **møte** | must |
| Obligation | ᛋᛣᚢᛚᛚᛖ | **skulle** | should |

## Adverbs and elatives

- Adverb-making suffix: **-ᛚᛁᛣ** / **-lik** (cf. English *-ly*)
- Elatives need ᛗᛖᚱ **mer** (more) and ᛗᛖᛋᛏ **mest** (most)
