# Caucas decisions

Design sketch for the Caucasus-region IAL (notes received 2026-10-26). Caucas is NOT one of the core fifteen and stays a single root page (`Caucas.md`) — user says low priority, no subtree planned.

## Settled

- Script: CAUCASIAN ALBANIAN (user decision 2026-10-26), letters assigned the same day. Native fits for most of the inventory; repurposed: 𐕃 (hist. dʑ)=/ɢ/, 𐕝 (ɕ)=/χ/, 𐔽 (ʕ)=/ʁ/; 𐕇=q only (historically [q~χ]). Vowels: i 𐔼, e 𐔴, a 𐔰, ə 𐕈 (hist. å [ɒ]), o 𐕒, u 𐕞 (hist. ü [y] — user chose the single letter over the historical o+w digraph 𐕒𐕡). Gemination = doubled letters. Loan reserve: 𐕆 h, 𐔶 ē, 𐕑 dʒ, 𐕕 dz, 𐕉 tɕ, 𐔹 tɕʼ, palatalized series 𐔿 𐕁 𐕅 𐕋 𐕏 𐕟 𐕓. Reference: Gippert's alphabet table (aiearmeniennes.org/artsakh/Alb-script/).
- Alignment: ergative, CONSISTENTLY — S=P (absolutive) vs A (ergative) across all tenses, no split.
- Cases: absolutive, ergative + genitive, dative, locative, allative, ablative, perlative, instrumental, comitative (10 total).
- Syntax: SOV with some flexibility; relative clauses precede their head noun.
- Categories: no gender, no evidentiality, no clusivity; T–V distinction present; polypersonal verbal agreement; clear agglutinative verbal morphology.
- Phonology: 6 vowels /i e a ə o u/ + gemination; weight-based predictable stress. Large consonant inventory with ejectives (pʼ tʼ kʼ qʼ, tsʼ tʃʼ), uvular series (q ɢ χ ʁ), but phonotactics deliberately below Georgian-level clustering.
- Sources (exhaustive list, user says it may need pruning):
  - Indigenous — Kartvelian: Georgian (primary reference), Mingrelian, Laz, Svan. Northwest Caucasian: Kabardian, Adyghe, Abkhaz, Abaza (+ historical Ubykh). Northeast Caucasian: Nakh (Chechen, Ingush, Batsbi); Avaro-Andi-Tsezic (Avar, Andi, Tsez, Karata, Akhvakh); Lezgic (Lezgi, Tabasaran, Aghul, Rutul, Tsakhur, Udi); Lak-Dargwa (Dargwa, Lak); isolate Khinalug.
  - Contact — Turkic: Azerbaijani, Karachay-Balkar, Kumyk, Nogai — these are ZONE MEMBERS OF ORTATÜRK (Azerbaijani was one of its five original 1992 averaging languages) and are areal contributors to Caucas only; shared, following the Romanian-between-Interlingua-and-Balkan precedent (boundary confirmed by user 2026-10-26). Indo-European: Armenian; Iranian (Ossetian Iron/Digor, Tat/Judeo-Tat, Talysh, Kurmanji Kurdish); Slavic (Russian, dominant regional lingua franca). The Iranian contact languages (Ossetian, Tat/Judeo-Tat, Talysh, Kurdish) are ZONE MEMBERS OF ZENS (pan-Iranian claims the whole Iranian branch) and areal contributors to Caucas only — user-confirmed 2026-10-26.
  - Weighting tiers: Tier 1 anchors = Georgian, Chechen, Avar, Azerbaijani, Armenian, Russian (lexical roots, internationalisms, macro-grammar). Tier 2 = Kabardian/Adyghe, Abkhaz, Lezgi, Ossetian, Kumyk, Karachay-Balkar (phonological constraints, local vocabulary, case/aspect nuance). Tier 3 = Mingrelian, Svan, Ingush, Lak, Dargwa, Tabasaran, Tat (verification of pan-Caucasian areal traits).
