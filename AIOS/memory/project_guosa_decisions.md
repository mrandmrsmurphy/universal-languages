# Guosa decisions

Guosa is an EXTANT project made by others (see Wikipedia), adopted as the vault's West African UL in its "Guosa 2.0" redesign (notes received 2026-10-25). Document the 2.0 design as given; note divergences rather than reforming.

## Settled

- Script: N'Ko, RIGHT-TO-LEFT. Combining mark ߳ voices the preceding letter (f→v, s→z, ɡ͡b→ɡ~ɣ); ʃ = digraph ߛߤ; marginal k͡p = ߞߔ; prenasalized stops written as nasal+stop digraphs (ᵐb ߡߓ, ⁿd ߣߘ, ⁿdʒ ߢߖ, ⁿtʃ ߢߗ, ᵑɡ ߒߜ߳, ᵑ͡ᵐɡ͡b ߒߜ).
- Phonology: CV/CVC; nasals m n ɲ ŋ; plosives p t tʃ k (k͡p), b d dʒ ɡ~ɣ ɡ͡b, plus prenasalized series; fricatives f s ʃ h~x v z; l j w ɹ (r marginal). Five vowels i~ɪ e~ɛ a o~ɔ u~ʊ — length and nasality NOT contrastive but writable for etymology (double vowels; tilde-like nasal mark ߱).
- Tone: two tones, high (marked, macron-like ߫: mó ߡߏ߫) and low (unmarked). Double vowels may split tones → HH/LL/HL/LH; falling ߮, rising ߭. Rationale: tones are hard even for tonal-language speakers, so keep it minimal.
- Grammar: analytic/isolating, SVO, no case, no gender, no conjugation (aspect via auxiliary particles, aspect > tense), serial verbs, clusivity, topicalization.
- Vocabulary: Yoruba, Igbo, Akan, Hausa (loanwords), Krio, Wolof — NO English-derived words. Proverbs and call-and-response are cultivated.
- Pronouns so far: 1sg mó ߡߏ߫.
