# Balkan decisions

Settled design for the Balkan sprachbund UL (notes received 2026-10-26).

## Settled

- Foundation: Balkan sprachbund; zone = Balkan peninsula. Member languages: Greek, (Ottoman) Turkish, Albanian, Serbian + the peninsula's South Slavic (absorbed from the Interslavic split); Romanian shared with Interlingua. Population ~60M.
- Name: Бαλκαν 'bal.kan. SVO, fusional, nominative–accusative.
- Script: Greek alphabet + Cyrillic Б/б for /b/ (Greek Β/β is /v/). Letter values cross Ancient and Modern Greek: η=/i/, β=/v/, φ=/f/, χ=/x/ modern; υ=/u/, γ=/g/, δ=/d/ ancient; ι=/j/ novel (iota is ONLY an approximant). Ω/ω repurposed as /ə/. Θ/θ foreign transcription only; Ξ/ξ=/ks/, Ψ/ψ=/ps/; sigma ς word-finally; τσ, δζ digraphs for the affricates.
- Phonology: m n; p t k, b d g; affricates ts~tʃ~tɕ and dz~dʒ~dʑ; f, s~ʃ, x~h, v~ʋ, z~ʒ; r~ɹ, j, l~ɫ (affricates/sibilants have alveolar-to-palatal realization ranges; /θ/ marginal). Six vowels i u e ə o~ɔ a — the region's five plus schwa from unstressed vowel reduction. Stress predictable, right-defined; the name is penult-stressed.
- Morphosyntax: nouns/adjectives inflect 3 genders × 3 cases (nominative, accusative, oblique) × 2 numbers; gender and case flatten in the plural. Oblique always governed by a preposition; vocative has a dedicated preposition. Definite article postposed; indefinite article preposed, a form of en 'one'. Pronoun paradigm: nom υζ/νη/τη/βη/σο/σα/το/λα; acc μει/νας/τει/βας/σου/σα/το/λα; obl μην/νεμ/την/βαμ/σεν/σαν/τον/λα, with reduced clitic forms after the arrows (με, να, τε, βα, σ'/τ', μη, νε, τη, βα, σε/σα/το). Numerals 1–10: en du tri star pan so eft okto inja des.
- Sprachbund features (hub + Anthropology): unstressed vowel reduction, loss of Slavic pitch accent, reduced case, postposed definite article, periphrastic future with modal verb, infinitives replaced by subordinate clauses, analytic comparative, dative clitic doubling, strong Ottoman Turkish vocabulary.
- Roadmap 2→3. Hub moved into Balkan/ (root stub removed); Universal Languages sample now Бαλκαν.
