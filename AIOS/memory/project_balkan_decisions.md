# Balkan decisions

Settled design for the Balkan sprachbund UL (notes received 2026-10-26).

## Settled

- Foundation: Balkan sprachbund; zone = Balkan peninsula. Member languages: Greek, (Ottoman) Turkish, Albanian, Serbian + the peninsula's South Slavic (absorbed from the Interslavic split); Romanian shared with Interlingua. Population ~60M.
- Name: Бαλκαν 'bal.kan. SVO, fusional, nominative–accusative.
- Script: Greek alphabet + Cyrillic Б/б for /b/ (Greek Β/β is /v/). Letter values cross Ancient and Modern Greek: η=/i/, β=/v/, φ=/f/, χ=/x/ modern; υ=/u/, γ=/g/, δ=/d/ ancient; ι=/j/ novel (iota is ONLY an approximant). Ω/ω repurposed as /ə/. Θ/θ foreign transcription only; Ξ/ξ=/ks/, Ψ/ψ=/ps/; sigma ς word-finally; τσ, δζ digraphs for the affricates.
- Phonology: m n; p t k, b d g; affricates ts~tʃ~tɕ and dz~dʒ~dʑ; f, s~ʃ, x~h, v~ʋ, z~ʒ; r~ɾ, j, l~ɫ (affricates/sibilants have alveolar-to-palatal realization ranges; /θ/ marginal). Six vowels i u e ə o~ɔ a — the region's five plus schwa from unstressed vowel reduction. Stress predictable, right-defined; the name is penult-stressed.
- Morphosyntax: nouns/adjectives inflect 3 genders × 3 cases (nominative, accusative, oblique) × 2 numbers; gender and case flatten in the plural. Oblique always governed by a preposition; vocative has a dedicated preposition. Definite article postposed; indefinite article preposed, a form of en 'one'. Pronoun paradigm: nom υζ/νη/τη/βη/σο/σα/το/λα; acc μει/νας/τει/βας/σου/σα/το/λα; obl μην/νεμ/την/βαμ/σεν/σαν/τον/λα, with reduced clitic forms after the arrows (με, να, τε, βα, σ'/τ', μη, νε, τη, βα, σε/σα/το). Numerals 1–10: en du tri star pan so eft okto inja des.
- Sprachbund features (hub + Anthropology): unstressed vowel reduction, loss of Slavic pitch accent, reduced case, postposed definite article, periphrastic future with modal verb, infinitives replaced by subordinate clauses, analytic comparative, dative clitic doubling, strong Ottoman Turkish vocabulary.
- Roadmap 2→3. Hub moved into Balkan/ (root stub removed); Universal Languages sample now Бαλκαν.

## Session 2026-11-02 (second review)

- Fixed: υ=/u/ documented as novel; d~ð and g~ɣ added as allophones (user OK); r range corrected to r~ɾ; numerals now spelled in Balkan orthography (εν δυ τρη σταρ παν σο εφτ οκτο ηνια δες) with Claude's source guesses (9 = colloquial Greek ennía; 4–6 blends; 6 'so' collides with 3sg masc pronoun σο).
- Pending: T–V system. Claude's suggestion: second-person plural (βη) as polite V — universal across Greek, Turkish, Albanian, Slavic; Romanian differs (dumneavoastră). Other flagged gaps (not yet actioned): evidentiality absent from the design though common in the sprachbund; genitive–dative merger and 'have'-perfect not listed; future built on 'want' (θα/do/ще) and subjunctive particle da (να would clash with 1pl clitic); stress rule needs a concrete form; /ɲ ʎ/ undecided; no source-language tiers (Romani, Aromanian, Gagauz, Judeo-Spanish absent); script rationale unrecorded.

- Sprachbund additions (user, 2026-11-02): T–V = 2pl βη as polite (optional capital Βη); small evidentiality system (one marker for reported/inferred/unexpected); oblique named as genitive–dative merger; perfect with 'have' + invariable participle; teens 'one-on-ten' with na (en na des…); future = particle da reduced from 'want'. Open: subjunctive/subordinate-clause marker (da again, or another); να 'us' vs na 'on' homophony; actual verb forms.

- Interview rulings (user, 2026-11-02): script rationale = every UL needs a unique script; Greek is a middle way between Latin and Cyrillic and an ancestor of both, missing only /b/ (hence Б). Members add Romani, Aromanian, Gagauz (Judeo-Spanish not added). Stress = fixed penult (name 'bal.kan consistent). /ɲ ʎ/ = sequences /nj lj/ (νι, λι), not phonemes. Consequence to watch: with fixed penult, unstressed reduction makes schwa largely predictable, so ω spelling policy (write reduced vowels or the underlying vowel) is unsettled.

- Rulings (user, 2026-11-02): schwa is not written except where absolutely necessary (very rare ω); da also marks subordinate clauses (replacing infinitives), main-clause da before the verb = future; limited homophony (να/na) accepted. Verb-form suggestions pending (endings -m -s -Ø -mo -te -n; want = dua; have = ima; be = regular e-; perfect = ima + -t participle; evidential = bare participle; neg ne; question li).

- Verb forms adopted (user, 2026-11-02): endings -m -s -∅ -mo -te -n; verbs vowel-final; want = dua, have = ima, be = regular e-; future = da + present; perfect = ima + invariable -t participle (only past, no aorist/imperfect); evidential = e- 'be' + participle (user's modification of Claude's 'bare participle' suggestion); da also opens subordinate clauses; negation ne before verb; polar question li after first word; imperative stem / -te. Aspect deferred by user.
