# MSEAL decisions

Settled design for the Mainland Southeast Asian sprachbund UL (first notes received 2026-10-25).

## Settled

- Foundation: sprachbund, not a genetic family. Bengali is in the zone (excluded from Neo-Sanskrit); also Khmer, Vietnamese, Hokkien/Hakka/Cantonese/SW Mandarin, Burmese, Kra-Dai (Tai, Lao, Shan, Kam), Chamic, Hmong, Mien.
- Script: New Tai Lue alphabet used as a TRUE ALPHABET, not an abugida. This supersedes the hub stub's earlier "Burmese (eventual)" plan; the old Burmese sample ဧသဃ was dropped from the hub.
- Phonology: monosyllabic morphemes; 4 tones (level unmarked, rising ᧈ, departing ᧉ, checked); CV/CVC only, no clusters; 7 codas p t k m n ŋ ʔ; vowels i e ə a o u + diphthongs ai au ei ou; consonant inventory with phonemic aspiration and voiced~implosive b~ɓ, d~ɗ.
- Tones and codas: areal standard — checked tone is confined to STOP-final syllables (-p -t -k -ʔ); open and nasal-final syllables are live and carry level/rising/departing. Tone letters co-occur with nasal coda letters (tone letter follows the coda). Matches Cantonese/Vietnamese/Thai/Burmese; no zone language treats nasal codas as checked.
- Sesquisyllables: words are Cə-CV or Cə-CVC; the minor syllable is Cə-, bears no tone, and takes no fricative onset. Cə- prefixes give abstract/grammatical meaning.
- Morphosyntax: isolating/analytic; word formation by Cə- prefixation, compounding, reduplication, serial verbs; aspect only, no tense; SVO with postnominal relative clauses; topic–comment; sentence-final particles for modality; pre-verbal negation particle; directional verbs; 5 sortal classifiers: human / animal / plant / object (default inanimate, absorbs the shape splits) / abstract (matters, events, kinds). Measure/group words remain an open class.
- Pronouns: 1sg ŋom; 1pl.incl ga, 1pl.excl ta; 2sg min, 2pl fe; 3 ket (sg=pl). Honorifics optional.
