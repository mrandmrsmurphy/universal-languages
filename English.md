# English

> Hub: [[Universal Languages]] · Germanic side: [[Folksprak]] · Romance side: [[Interlingua]]

**English** is the 800-pound elephant of the project: the largest language on Earth by total speakers (~400 million native, over a billion total) and the de facto international auxiliary language already in use. It is also, structurally, two languages in one coat — a Germanic skeleton wearing a Romance–Classical wardrobe, a Frankenstein's monster that never quite fused. That double nature makes English the living model for the [[Folksprak]]–[[Interlingua]] choice.

## Anatomy of a hybrid

- The **grammar and core vocabulary** are Germanic: the function words, the strong verbs, the everyday nouns (*house, hand, water, eat, go*), the word-order bones.
- Layers on top, in order of arrival:
  1. **Old Norse** (Danelaw, 9th–11th c.): *sky, skin, leg, they/them/their* — so basic they go unnoticed.
  2. **Norman French** (after 1066): government (*crown, parliament, court*), cuisine (*beef, pork, mutton* — the French word is the meat, the Germanic word the animal), law, art, fashion.
  3. **Latin** (church and scholarship): scripture, legal formulas, abstract nouns.
  4. **Greek and Latin compounding** (science, 16th c. onward): *television, photograph, biology*.

## The register split is a class split

English sorts its double (and triple) vocabulary by prestige. Broadly: plain, everyday, "uneducated" English is Germanic; polysyllabic, snobbish English is Romance.

| Germanic (folk) | French (polite) | Latin/Greek (learned) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| kingly | royal | regal |
| ask | question | interrogate |
| rise | mount | ascend |
| hearty | cordial | cardiac |
| begin | commence | initiate |
| think | ponder | cogitate |

Legal doublets show the two halves stapled together: *will and testament*, *free and clear*, *null and void*.

## The conlang experiments

Conlangers have run the separation in both directions, and both directions work — proof the halves never fused:

- **Anglish** strips English back to its Germanic roots: Poul Anderson's *Uncleftish Beholding* renders atomic theory with *uncleft* (atom), *bulkbits* (particles), *waterstuff* (hydrogen), *sourstuff* (oxygen).
- **Anglese** (a [romconlang project](http://ygarchive.conlang.org/romconlang/messages/msg02596.html)) runs the other way — English re-Romanicized. (Confusingly, *anglese* is also simply the Interlingua word for English.)
- **Britannian** ([documentation](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1VSRNb0GJl_bHr3s4cnAbf2hkQSJpO5LhjTzg8ULvD2c/edit)) describes itself as "based on the common misconception that English is a Romance language": a western Romance language descended from the Vulgar Latin of Roman Britannia, which in its timeline replaced both the Brythonic dialects and Old English, absorbing heavy Anglo-Saxon, Old Norse, and Old Norman influence. Modeled on the northern langues d'Oïl (Norman, Picard, Walloon — the most Germanic-flavoured Romance varieties), it differs from Brithenig (which leans Brythonic) and Britainese. In its world it is the official language of *le United Reign* and *les United States d'America*, and reads exactly like English fed through a Romance filter: *Tots human essences nashen libres e equals en dignity e drites.*

## What this means for the UL project

- The Folksprak-vs-Interlingua question in the English-speaking world is not arbitrary; it replays English's own internal split. The Folksprak zone claims the Germanic half, the Interlingua zone the Romance half — and the social register split (plain vs. learned) maps onto it almost exactly.
- Zoning as settled so far: English itself defaults to **Folksprak**, which also absorbs Afrikaans (e.g. South Africa); much of Hispanic America leans **Interlingua**. See [[Folksprak/Anthropology]] and [[Interlingua/Anthropology]].
- English speakers get a passive discount on both ULs: the Germanic core makes Folksprak half-familiar at sight, and the Latinate stratum makes Interlingua half-readable. This is also why English could serve as a primary control language in Interlingua's original design — its vocabulary already contains the international Romance stock.
- The same is roughly true in miniature elsewhere (Danish with its Low German stratum, Persian-flavoured Hindustani), but nowhere else is the split as total or as load-bearing as in English.
