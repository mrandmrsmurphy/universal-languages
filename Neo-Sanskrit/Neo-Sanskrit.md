Most languages in the region passed through Prakrits into Hindustani, Punjabi, Kashmiri, Garhwali, Rajasthani, Maithili, and many more. Bengali is deliberately excluded — it would break too many universals of the remaining group — and belongs with [[MSEAL]]. Dravidian languages shaped phonology but remain a separate UL ([[Dravindian]]).

## Uniting features

- Clipped Sanskrit — native words as shortened Sanskrit forms
- Collapsing phonology — four stop series × five places simplified differently per language
- Two genders
- Postpositions
- Split ergativity
