# Phonology

> Parent: [[Neo-Sanskrit]] · Script: [[Neo-Sanskrit/Orthography]]

The Sanskrit stop grid — four series by five places — is kept whole, which is the one thing no modern Indo-Aryan language quite does.

## Consonants

| Manner | Labial | Dental | Retroflex | Palatal | Velar |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Voiceless | /p/ | /t̪/ | /ʈ/ | /tʃ~tɕ~c/ | /k/ |
| Voiced | /b/ | /d̪/ | /ɖ/ | /dʒ/ | /ɡ/ |
| Aspirated | /pʰ/ | /t̪ʰ/ | /ʈʰ/ | /tʃʰ/ | /kʰ/ |
| Breathy | /bʱ/ | /d̪ʱ/ | /ɖʱ/ | /dʒʱ/ | /ɡʱ/ |
| Nasal | /m/ | /n/ | /ɳ~ɲ~ŋ/ | | |
| Fricative | /f~ʋ~w/ | /s/ | /ʂ/ | /ʃ~ɕ~ɬ/ | /h~ɦ/ |
| Liquid | | /l/ | /ɾ/ | /j/ | |

- **Breathy consonants are written apart from the plain voiced series, but no minimal pairs may exist between them.** The breathy series is kept for recognition across the region; the contrast is never load-bearing.
- The retroflex, palatal, and velar nasals are a single phoneme with positional variants (see [[Neo-Sanskrit/Orthography]] — three letters, one sound).
- /f~ʋ~w/ (written va) and /h~ɦ/ (ha) are marginal.

## Vowels

- a, e, i, o, u, ṛ (syllabic r survives as a vowel)
- ā, ī, ū
- ai, au

There is no syllabic l: Sanskrit ḷ became li.

## Phonotactics

Syllable shapes: V, CV, CCV, CVC, CCVC, CVCC, CCVCC — at most two consonants per margin.
