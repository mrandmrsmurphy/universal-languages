# Morphosyntax

> Parent: [[Neo-Sanskrit]] · Sounds: [[Neo-Sanskrit/Phonology]] · Script: [[Neo-Sanskrit/Orthography]]

Word order is **SOV**, as throughout the region.

## Gender

Gender is read straight off the word's final sound — no lexical gender to memorize:

- Ends in a consonant → **masculine**
- Ends in a vowel → **feminine**; final **-e** is forbidden on gendered words

(Postpositions and other particles ending in -e, like ne and me, sit outside the gender system.)

## Alignment and case

Split ergativity by transitivity, marked by postpositions:

- The subject of an **intransitive** clause is unmarked.
- The subject of a **transitive** clause takes the ergative postposition.
- The object of a transitive clause takes the accusative postposition.
