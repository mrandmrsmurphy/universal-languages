**Middle Semitic** is a Universal Language for the Middle East and North Africa. It is a blend of Hebrew and Arabic, written in Syriac.

## The middle-semitic vault

Middle Semitic has its own Obsidian vault at `../middle-semitic` (`/Users/robertmarshallmurphy/Documents/middle-semitic`), where all real work happens — spun out so its lexicon entries can carry per-tradition cognate properties (Ugaritic, Akkadian, Geʿez…) as YAML frontmatter. It is the second language to graduate, and the largest of the in-house projects:

- **450 lexicon entries**, one file per word with full paradigms (strong/weak/geminate/hollow verbs, three noun declensions, adjectives, conjugated prepositions), plus **284 root pages**
- Lexicon entries carry YAML frontmatter with open-ended cognate properties (Hebrew, Arabic, Aramaic, Ugaritic, Akkadian, Proto-Semitic…)
- Complete grammar: phonology, Syriac orthography, morphophonology, morphosyntax, predictable stress
- Comparative Swadesh page (MSA, Hebrew, Syriac, Egyptian, Palestinian), 128 of 207 MS cells filled
- Texts: a dialogue, the Shahada, the Jesus Prayer, and a word-by-word Torah project (Genesis 1:1, the Shema)
- Its own AIOS memory system and generator scripts; git-tracked. The [Google Sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1n6hf5apPWqBziInberzWIOfPXMQ6jVaz_hCbS5PnoF8/edit?usp=sharing) remains the source record for the imported lexicon

## Uniting features

- Triconsonantal root morphology shared by Hebrew, Arabic, and Aramaic
- A neutral script: Syriac, equally distant from the Hebrew and Arabic alphabets
- Diglossia mirrored from both source cultures (a prestige classical stratum and an everyday one)
- Jews, Muslims, and Christians get an equal vote; where the sources split, a logical compromise rather than a side
