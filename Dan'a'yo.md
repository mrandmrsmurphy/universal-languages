The first and biggest UL. Unites Mandarin, Japanese, Korean, and (to some extent) Vietnamese speakers — not a genetic family, but a shared Sinospheric history and Classical Chinese legacy.

## The danayo vault

Dan'a'yo has its own Obsidian vault at `../danayo` (`/Users/robertmarshallmurphy/Documents/danayo`), where all real work happens. It is the furthest along of the fifteen ULs by a wide margin:

- **~3,370 character pages** (`characters/`) — Han characters only, never words; a character that can also stand alone is filed as `X (char).md` so it doesn't collide with the word `words/X.md`
- **~6,550 word pages** (`words/`) — the actual vocabulary, mostly compounds (words outnumber characters ~2:1)
- **~900 syllable pages** (the Bopomofo phonetic inventory) and **~240 chengyu** (idioms with source citations verified against the named texts)

(Live counts: `danayo/AIOS/facts.yaml` — the reportage-protocol front door, regenerated by script; the numbers above are approximations for readers.)
- All four folders fully audited ("perfected"); no backlog
- A **16-chapter grammar** (単亜語之文法書) with English mirrors for chapters 1–5; the pragmatics chapter is still a skeleton and the planned reader doesn't exist yet
- A **lexipedia** (domain-organized vocabulary, including the comparative Swadesh table: Old Chinese, Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese, Dan'a'yo), lookup trees (radicals, SKIP, stroke counts, Classical Chinese initials/finals, HSK/Jōyō/Korean school levels), and translations of classical texts (Analects, Zhuangzi, Dao De Jing, Art of War...), many fragmentary
- **Lessons are the open frontier**: 3 of 20 planned lessons written; the vault self-rates ~85% sufficient as a reference, ~40% for learners

The vault runs its own AIOS memory system (the model for this vault's), with Templater templates, Obsidian Bases, and Python linters keeping the corpus consistent.

## Uniting features

- Topic–comment structure (more important than SVO vs SOV)
- Classifiers
- Aspirated vs unaspirated stops
- Shared classical texts (Confucius, Lao-Tzu, Mencius, Zhuangzi, etc.)
