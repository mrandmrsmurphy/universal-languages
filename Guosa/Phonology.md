Parenthesized phonemes (k͡p, r) are marginal. The prenasalized series and the labiovelars ɡ͡b (and marginal k͡p) are characteristic West African sounds, kept for recognition in source vocabulary.

## Vowels

Five vowels, each spanning a range: i~ɪ, e~ɛ, a, o~ɔ, u~ʊ. Vowel length is not contrastive, but may be written (by doubling) to show etymology — see tone below. Nasality is not contrastive either, but may be written to show the etymology of foreign words.

## Tone

Two tones, high and low. Low is unmarked. Two like vowels in a row may carry different tones, so under an alternative analysis Guosa 2.0 has long and short vowels with six melodies: H, L, HH, LL, HL (falling), LH (rising).

Tone is deliberately minimal: even speakers of tonal languages struggle to learn new tones.

## Syllables

CV and CVC.
