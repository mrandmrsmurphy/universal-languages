# Orthography

> Parent: [[Guosa]]

Guosa 2.0 is written in N'Ko, which is written right-to-left.

The combining mark ߳ voices the letter it follows: ߝ f → ߝ߳ v, ߛ s → ߛ߳ z, ߜ ɡ͡b → ߜ߳ ɡ~ɣ. /ʃ/ is the digraph ߛߤ (s+h), and marginal /k͡p/ is ߞߔ (k+p). Prenasalized stops are written as digraphs with the corresponding nasal.

## Vowels

| | Letter |
| --- | --- |
| i~ɪ | ߌ |

| a | ߊ |
| o~ɔ | ߏ (ߐ for ɔ) |
| u~ʊ | ߎ |

The tight/wide variants (ߍ ɛ, ߐ ɔ) may be written to show etymology, as may double vowels for (non-contrastive) length. A nasalization mark resembling a tilde (߱) writes foreign nasal vowels, also non-contrastive.

## Tone marks

Low tone is unmarked. High tone takes a macron-like mark (߫): mó = ߡߏ߫. Falling tone is ߮ and rising tone is ߭, written on double vowels.
