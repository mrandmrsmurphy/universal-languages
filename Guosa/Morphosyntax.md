# Morphosyntax

> Parent: [[Guosa]]

Guosa 2.0 is analytic and isolating: grammatical relations depend on word order, and there is no case, no gender, and no extensive verb conjugation.

- **SVO word order** — aligns with many West African languages and with the English widely spoken in Nigeria.
- **Aspect over tense** — instead of conjugation, auxiliary particles mark aspect.
- **Serial verbs**.
- **Clusivity** — inclusive vs. exclusive "we".
- **Topicalization**.
