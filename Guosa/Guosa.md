Neutral means of communication across Hausa, Mande, Yoruba, Igbo, and related communities.

## Uniting features

- Analytic / isolating typology, word-order dependent
- SVO
- Little verb conjugation — aspect (not tense) via auxiliary particles
- Serial verbs
- No gender, no case
- Clusivity
- Topicalization
- Five vowels, two tones (high/low)
- CV and CVC syllables
- Vocabulary from Yoruba, Igbo, Akan, Hausa (loanwords), Krio, Wolof — no English
- Proverbs and call-and-response forms
