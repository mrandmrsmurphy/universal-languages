# Anthropology

> Parent: [[Guosa]]

![[West_Africa_Flag.svg\|180]]

Guosa is an extant auxlang project for West Africa (external project, adopted here in its "2.0" redesign) — a neutral means of communication across Hausa, Mande, Yoruba, Igbo, and related communities. See [Guosa (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guosa).

## Vocabulary sources

Words are favored from Yoruba, Igbo, Akan, Hausa (loanwords), Krio, and Wolof. English-derived words are deliberately excluded.

## Style

Proverbs and call-and-response forms are cultivated features of the language.
