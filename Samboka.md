**Samboka** is a reconstructed Uralic auxiliary language designed for maximum simplicity, regularity, and broad cross-recognition across all branches of the Uralic family — not Finnic-only.

## Alphabet and phonology

Phonetic spelling: one letter, one sound. Latin with diacritics for distinct Uralic phonemes.

**A Ä B C Č D E F G H I J K L M N O Ö P R S Š T U Ü V Z Ž**

- **Ü** — French *u* / German *ü* /y/
- **C** — /ts/
- **Č** — /tʃ/
- **Š** — /ʃ/
- **Z** — /z/
- **Ž** — /ʒ/
- **G** — always hard /ɡ/, including in *ng*

### Vowel harmony

Strict. A stem has either **back** vowels (*A, O, U*) or **front** (*Ä, Ö, Ü*). *E* and *I* are neutral and may co-occur with either set.

## Word classes

Nouns, adjectives, verbs, adverbs, numerals, particles.

## Nouns

### Number

Singular (base) and plural. Plural marker **-i-** on the invariant stem.

- *kala* (fish) → *kalai* (fishes)
- *kodo* (home) → *kodoi* (homes)
- *käd* (hand) → *kädi* (hands)

### Case system (10)

Regular 10-case system: a clean **3×3** of spatial/state relations × directions, plus **comitative / abessive**. The old approximative series is left to postpositions; state transformations are folded into the essive triad. Stems never mutate; suffixes attach to vowel stems, or via buffer **-e-** after consonants.

Directional endings:

- **-e** — motion towards (illative / sublative / translative)
- **-a / -ä** — static location or state (inessive / superessive / essive)
- **-o / -ö** — motion away (elative / delative / exessive)

| Case | Suffix (back / front) | Function | Singular | Plural |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nominative | — | Subject | *kala* | *kalai* |
| Accusative | -t | Direct object | *kalat* | *kalait* |
| Genitive | -n | Possession | *kalan* | *kalain* |
| Internal local | -se / -sa / -sto | Into / inside / out of | *kalase* / *kalasa* / *kalasto* | *kalaise* / *kalaisa* / *kalaisto* |
| External local | -le / -la / -lto | Onto / on / off of | *kalale* / *kalala* / *kalalto* | *kalaile* / *kalaila* / *kalailto* |
| State / essive | -kse / -ksa / -kso | Becoming / being / ceasing to be | *kalakse* / *kalaksa* / *kalakso* | *kalaikse* / *kalaiksa* / *kalaikso* |
| Comitative | -ga / -gä | With (alongside) | *kalaga* | *kalaiga* |
| Abessive | -ta / -tä | Without | *kalata* | *kalaita* |

## Vocabulary (pan-Uralic roots)

Core lexicon draws from Ugric and Samoyedic as well as Finnic.

- **käd** — hand (Fin. *käsi*, Hung. *kéz*, Nenets *ngoda*)
- **seńa** — eye (Hung. *szem*, Mansi *säm*, Nenets *sew*)
- **vete** — water (Fin. *vesi*, Hung. *víz*, Selkup *ut*)
- **elä-** — to live (Fin. *elää*, Hung. *él*, Selkup *elä-*)

## Pronouns

### Personal

| Person | Singular | Plural |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st | *me* (I) | *mei* (we) |
| 2nd | *te* (you) | *tei* (you all) |
| 3rd | *se* (he/she/it) | *sei* (they) |

Pronouns take standard case suffixes: *män* (my), *mät* (me [acc.]), *mële* (to me).

### Demonstratives and interrogatives

- *ti* — this
- *to* — that
- *mi* — what
- *ki* — who
- *jo* — which / relative

## Postpositions

Same directional vowel rule as cases. Preceding noun stays nominative: *vete ala* (under water), *maja sisa* (inside the house).

| Towards (-e) | At (-a/-ä) | Away (-o/-ö) | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *sise* | *sisa* | *siso* | inside |
| *ede* | *eda* | *edo* | in front of |
| *tage* | *taga* | *tago* | behind |
| *ale* | *ala* | *alo* | under |
| *pele* | *pelä* | *pelö* | above / on top |

## Verbs and word order

### SOV

Default **Subject–Object–Verb**, matching historical Proto-Uralic and modern Hungarian, Khanty, Nenets.

- *Me kalat sööm.* — I eat [the] fish. (I fish-ACC eat-1SG)
- *Se maja sise läktäs.* — He goes into the house. (He house inside goes-3SG)

### Non-past

Personal agreement on the invariant stem:

| Person | Singular | Plural |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st | *me eläm* | *mei elämi* |
| 2nd | *te elät* | *tei eläti* |
| 3rd | *se eläs* | *sei eläsi* |

### Past

Tense marker **-i-** before personal suffixes: *me eläim*, *te eläit*, *se eläis*.

### Potential mood

**-ne-**: *Mei homena läktänemi.* — We will probably leave tomorrow.

### Negation

Two historic Uralic patterns allowed:

1. Inflecting negative verb (Finnic/Samoyedic): *Me nem puna* — I do not weave
2. Invariant particle (Ugric/Estonian): *Me ne punam* — I do not weave

## Derivation

Fixed final vowels on roots:

| Ending | Role | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **-a / -ä** | Transitive verb | *murda* — to break (tr.) |
| **-u / -ü** | Intransitive verb | *murdu* — to break (intr.) |
| **-e** | Result noun | *murde* — a fracture |
| **-o / -ö** | Process noun | *murdo* — breaking / burglary |
| **-i** | Adjective | *murdi* — broken |
