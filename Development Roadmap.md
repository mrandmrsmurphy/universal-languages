# Development Roadmap

> Hub: [[Universal Languages]] · Compare: [[Language Comparisons]]

A 1–10 milestone scale for developing a Universal Language, expanded from the six-phase sketch: boundaries → commonalities → vocabulary → sentences → vocabulary at scale → translation. The phases overlap and any of them can be started at any time; the compromises a given UL makes will alter the particulars. A language's score is the highest milestone **fully reached**.

A language graduates to **its own vault** when it outgrows incubation here — crossing into 7 territory, or needing a richer format than the shared one supports (Middle Semitic: per-tradition cognate properties). Dan'a'yo lives at `/Users/robertmarshallmurphy/Documents/danayo`, Middle Semitic at `/Users/robertmarshallmurphy/Documents/middle-semitic`. Until then each language is incubated here with the standard subtree.

## The scale

| Score | Milestone | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Boundaries settled** | Which lects are in the group and which are out is decided, with reasons (e.g. Bengali moved from Neo-Sanskrit to MSEAL because it would break too many universals of the remaining group). The map reflects the decision. |
| 2 | **Phonology** | The phoneme inventory and phonotactics are fixed from commonalities — including things-common-enough-that-everyone-learns-them-anyway (e.g. Folksprak's front-rounded vowels, absent from English and Yiddish). An orthography exists, interim script acceptable. |
| 3 | **Morphology** | Word classes, inflection, and the derivational patterns are fixed. |
| 4 | **Syntax** | Word order, alignment, and clause structure are fixed — a complete reference grammar exists on paper. |
| 5 | **Core lexicon** | The Swadesh 207 and Leipzig-Jakarta lists are filled with common and derivable forms, drawn from whatever shared stratum the group has (proto-language, classical language, or sprachbund consensus). |
| 6 | **Sentences** | The conlanger's 218 (or equivalent) are translated; the derivational, syntactic, and grammatical gaps they expose are flushed out and fixed. First dialogues and texts written. |
| 7 | **Working lexicon** | ~1,000+ words. Word-formation guidelines are proven by use; derivation is productive. |
| 8 | **Full lexicon** | 3,000+ words. The Lexipedia is complete across its domains. |
| 9 | **Literature** | Translation of important works from the source languages is substantial. |
| 10 | **Teachable** | Lessons and learner materials exist; a new speaker can actually acquire the language from them. |
