> Parent: [[Indo-Malay]]

## Pronouns

The pronoun system is the fullest expression of Indo-Malay's uniting features: clusivity (inclusive vs. exclusive "we"), a three-way number contrast (singular, dual, plural), politeness (informal vs. formal), and a human/non-human split in the third person.

Spelling conventions used: vowel-initial syllables and vowel hiatus take the ᨕ carrier (ti.e.ta = ᨈᨗᨕᨙᨈ); nasal codas take the ᨛ virama (lun = ᨒᨘᨊᨛ). Where a hiatus ᨕ onsets a nasal-final syllable it is pronounced as a true glottal stop, keeping every syllable inside the V/CV/CVN canon: pien = pi.ʔen ᨄᨗᨕᨙᨊᨛ, lian = li.ʔan ᨒᨗᨕᨊᨛ.

Visible patterning: the duals of the 2nd and 3rd persons contain rua "two"; the politeness element pien recurs across formal forms; and formal plurals show reduplication (nipienieta, liliu). Third-person formal and non-human forms do not distinguish dual from plural.

The set keeps the Malay/Indonesian semantics, including the reason/purpose split for "why" (mengapa vs. untuk apa). Etymological notes: **kiniu** is the shared "why" base < colloquial *kenapa*, with **ngu-** < *meng(apa)* for reason and **tu-** < *untuk* "for" for purpose; the *where* series is transparently preposition + **mina** < *mana* (dari > nure drops the d, though d exists in the inventory); **nianu** contains *anu*, the Indonesian "whatchamacallit"; **kuja** compresses *bagaimana* (cf. Sundanese *kumaha*). The recurring **-niu** element (ngukiniu, tukiniu, siniu, niruniu, nianu) is a potential question-formative morpheme.

Two proposed forms were respelled to fit the V/CV/CVN canon: tukkiniu → **tukiniu** (degeminated; k is not a legal coda) and nirniu → **niruniu** (vowel inserted; r is not a legal coda).

## Etymology of the pronouns

The system is built from real Austronesian material:

- **syu** "you" < PMP \*Su (cf. Tagalog *ikaw* < \*i kaSu, Malay *engkau*), while **sida** "they" < PMP \*siDa (cf. Old Javanese *sida*) with plain s — the pronoun table itself encodes the PMP \*S/\*s split.
- **nya** "he/she" < Malay *-nya*, the 3rd-person enclitic (human), vs. **ya** "it" < PMP \*ia (non-human).
- **tieta** (1pl incl) < PMP \*kita; **nimi** (1pl excl) < PMP \*kami, remodeled to the ni- pattern.
- **ikia**, **iki-** (1st person) contain \*aku (PAn nominative \*i-aku).
- **rua** (dual) < \*duha "two" with an Oceanic-style r-reflex (cf. Māori *rua*).
- **nalian** (you all) < Malay *kalian*; **liu** (formal he/she) < Malay *beliau*; **lun** (in ikilun, formal I) < Old Javanese *ulun*, the humble 1st person.
- **pien** (politeness) has no clear source; possibly coined (a distant echo of Javanese *panjenengan*?). Syllabified pi.ʔen.
