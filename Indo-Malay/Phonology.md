The palatals ɲ (ny) and ɕ (sy) were added to account for the pronoun forms: sy continues PMP \*Su "you" while plain s continues \*s (sida < \*siDa), so the s/sy contrast is etymological.

## Vowels

Six vowels: i, e, a, o, u, and ə/ø. The sixth vowel is written ᨛ, which also serves as a virama, glottal marker, and cluster indicator (see [[Indo-Malay/Orthography]]).

## Syllables

Syllables are V, CV, or CVN — a nasal is the only permitted coda.
