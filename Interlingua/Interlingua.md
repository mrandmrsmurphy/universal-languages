Serves French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, and related languages. Romanian is shared with [[Balkan]] by design — genetically Romance, but a core member of the Balkan sprachbund (like Sudan overlapping [[Middle Semitic]]). English influenced Interlingua's design (it is an IALA control language) but is not part of the zone it serves. An excellent regional compromise; preference would have been to keep two genders (as source languages do), but the project stands as-is.
