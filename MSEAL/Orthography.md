# Orthography

> Parent: [[MSEAL]]

MSEAL uses the New Tai Lue alphabet, but as a true alphabet rather than an abugida — a novel way to use an existing script. This is more familiar to neighbors who use the Latin script and are accustomed to writing everything down.

## Tone letters

| level | rising | departing | checked |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| — | ᧈ | ᧉ | stop coda letter |

The checked tone is not written with a tone letter at all: a stop coda letter (ᧇ ᧆ ᧅ ᧁ) marks it. Level is unmarked. On live syllables with a nasal coda, the tone letter follows the coda letter (e.g., ᦂᦱᦃᧈ kan + rising).
