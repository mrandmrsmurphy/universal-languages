Fantastically diverse: Vietnamese, Thai, Cantonese, Khmer, Hmong, Bengali, and others — bound by areal features rather than common descent.

## Uniting features

- Sesquisyllabicity: a "minor syllable" before the stressed main syllable
- Four tones; CV/CVC syllables, no clusters
- Isolating/analytic morphology: almost no inflection or derivation, meaning expressed via word order, particles, and compounding
- Classifiers required when counting nouns (human, animal, object, abstract)
- Serial verb constructions: verb chains without overt conjunctions
- Aspect instead of tense: post-verbal aspectual markers and sentence-final particles
- Pronoun systems with honorifics, based on politeness and familiarity
- Topic–comment structure
- SVO order with heavy postnominal modification
- Sentence-final particles for modality, politeness, evidentiality, and discourse
- Dedicated pre-verbal negation particles
- Reduplication for pluralization, intensity, or aspect
- Directional verbs: motion verbs specify direction explicitly
- Many Sino-Buddhist loanwords
