# Anthropology

> Parent: [[MSEAL]]

![[MSEAL.png\|180]]

MSEAL covers a fantastically diverse zone — Vietnamese, Thai, Cantonese, Khmer, Hmong, Bengali, and others — bound by areal features rather than common descent (see the [Mainland Southeast Asia linguistic area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mainland_Southeast_Asia_linguistic_area)). It is a sprachbund UL, not a genetic family.

## Languages

- Indo-Aryan
  - Bengali
- Austroasiatic
  - Khmer
  - Vietnamese
- Sino-Tibetan
  - Chinese: Hokkien, Hakka, Cantonese, Southwestern Mandarin
  - Burmese
- Kra-Dai
  - Tai, Lao, Shan, Kam
- Austronesian
  - Chamic
- Hmong-Mien
  - Hmong
  - Mien

## Lexicon

The zone shares a deep layer of Sino-Buddhist loanwords, a natural reservoir for learned and abstract vocabulary.
