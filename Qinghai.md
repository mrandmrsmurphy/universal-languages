> Not one of the core fifteen ULs, and smaller in usage than [[Caucas]]. Hub: [[Universal Languages]] · Single-page project, lowest priority — no subtree planned.

A sketch for a sprachbund UL of the Qinghai region (see the [Qinghai–Gansu sprachbund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qinghai%E2%80%93Gansu_sprachbund)), where Tibetic, Mongolic, Turkic and Sinitic languages have converged.

## Shared features

The convergent traits the design is built on:

- **SOV** word order, firmly.
- **Postpositions** and **suffixing** morphology throughout.
- **No person or number marking on the verb.**
- **Case marking**, notably a merged accusative/dative.
- **Dative possessor construction.**
- **Non-finite and converb constructions** for subordination.
- **Plain vs. aspirated** stop contrast.
- **No tone.**
- **Simple phonotactics** — no high-complexity clusters.

## Phonology target

A mid-sized consonant inventory, based on the areal pattern. The inventory can be written entirely in ʼPhags-pa.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Stops | plain and aspirated at p, t, k and q |
| Affricates | ts and tʃ, each plain and aspirated |
| Fricatives | s, ʃ, x or χ, h, f |
| Nasals | m, n, ŋ |
| Liquids and glides | l, r, j, w |

Plain vs. aspirated is the only laryngeal contrast; there is no tone and no complex clustering.
