Persian, Pashto, Kurdish, Balochi, and related languages — a pan-Iranian auxiliary for the Persosphere. The umbrella covers the entire Iranian branch, including the Caucasus Iranian languages (Ossetian, Tat, Talysh), which are shared with [[Caucas]] as areal contributors there.

## Uniting features

- Tripartite alignment — compromise between nominative–accusative and split-ergative sources
- Evidentiality (direct / hearsay / inferential)
- Five tense-aspect suffixes
- Reduplication with category-dependent meanings
- Six vowels including the low pair æ~a / ɒ
