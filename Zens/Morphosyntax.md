# Morphosyntax

> Parent: [[Zens]]

Zens is SOV and agglutinative.

## Alignment

Tripartite morphosyntactic alignment — a compromise between the nominative–accusative and split-ergative sources of the zone:

- **Nominative** (intransitive subject): unmarked
- **Accusative**: -ra
- **Ergative** (transitive subject): -(y)a

| -ro | future |

| -da | perfect |

| ø | direct |

## Reduplication

Reduplication's meaning depends on the word class:

- **Nouns**: "all over the place" (scattered plurality)
- **Adjectives**: "very" (intensive)
- **Adverbs**: distributive ("each")
- **Verbs** (partial reduplication): habitual / repetitive
