**Interslavic** (Medžuslovjansky, Меджусловйанскы) is the pan-Slavic Universal Language, built to let speakers of the various Slavic languages — and non-Slavs who want to reach them — communicate with instant mutual intelligibility. South Slavic is a non-voting audience: it votes in the [[Balkan]] sprachbund UL instead.
