# Morphology

> Parent: [[Interslavic]]

The o/e y/i rule is in [[Interslavic/Morphosyntax]].

## Nouns

Interslavic is an inflecting language. Nouns can have three genders, two numbers (singular and plural), and six cases: nominative, accusative, genitive, dative, instrumental and locative. Since several Slavic languages also have a vocative, it is displayed in the tables as well, even though strictly speaking the vocative is not a case. It occurs only in the singular of masculine and feminine nouns.

There is no article. The complicated system of noun classes in Slavic has been reduced to four declensions:

1. masculine nouns, ending in a (usually hard) consonant: dom "house", muž "man"
2. feminine nouns ending in -a: žena "woman", zemja "earth"
3. feminine nouns ending in a consonant, which is soft: kost "bone"
4. neuter nouns ending in -o or -e: slovo "word", morje "sea"

Each table cell gives the Latin form, then the Cyrillic form, which is the final orthography. Whether a noun is hard or soft, and whether it is animate, is a property of the word and is not marked in the spelling.

## Adjectives

Adjectives are always regular. They agree with the noun they modify in gender, case and number, and are usually placed before it. In the masculine columns, the first relates to animate nouns and the second to inanimate nouns. A distinction is made between hard and soft stems, for example *dobry* (добры) "good" and *sveži* (свежи) "fresh".

### Comparison

The comparative is formed with the ending **-(ej)ši**: *slabši* (слабши) "weaker", *polnejši* (полнейши) "fuller". The superlative is formed from the comparative with the prefix **naj-**: *najslabši* (найслабши) "weakest". Comparatives can also be formed with the adverbs *bolje* (болє) or *vyše* (выше) "more", superlatives with the adverbs *najbolje* (найболє) or *najvyše* (найвыше) "most".

### Adverbs

Hard adjectives can be turned into an adverb with the ending **-o**, soft adjectives with the ending **-e**: *dobro* (добро) "well", *sveže* (свеже) "freshly". Comparatives and superlatives can be adverbialized with the ending **-eje**: *slabeje* (слабеє) "weaker".

## Pronouns

The personal pronouns are *ja* (я) "I", *ty* (ты) "you, thou", *on* (он) "he", *ona* (она) "she", *ono* (оно) "it", *my* (мы) "we", *vy* (вы) "you" (pl.), and *oni* (они) / *one* (оне) "they". Each cell gives the Latin form, then the Cyrillic form; short forms are in parentheses.

### Other pronouns

Other pronouns are inflected as adjectives (see [[Interslavic/Morphology#Adjectives]]).

Possessive:

- **moj** (мой) "my"
- **tvoj** (твой) "your, thy"
- **naš** (наш) "our"
- **vaš** (ваш) "your (pl.)"
- **svoj** (свой) "my/your/his/her/our/their own"
- **čij** (чий) "whose"

Demonstrative:

- **toj** (той) "this, that"
- **tutoj** (тутой) "this"
- **tamtoj** (тамтой) "that"

Relative, interrogative and indefinite:

- **ktory** (кторы) "which"
- **kto** (кто) "who"
- **čto** (что) "what"
- **nekto** (некто) "somebody"
- **nečto** (нечто) "something"
- **nikto** (никто) "nobody"
- **ničto** (ничто) "nothing"
- **ktokoli** (ктоколи) "whoever, anybody"
- **čto-nebud** (что-небуд) "whatever, anything"
