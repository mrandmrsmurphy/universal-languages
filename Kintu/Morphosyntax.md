# Morphosyntax

> Parent: [[Kintu]]

Kintu is SVO, agglutinative and heavily prefixing, with nominative–accusative alignment.

## Noun classes

Old Proto-Bantu 12–13 (diminutives etc.) differ too much across the region to keep. Classes above 17 are disputed. Classes 5–6 fold into 7–8; 11 into 14; 16 and 18 into 17.

## Verbs

| Tense | Marker | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Present | Ø | *londa* — follows / is following |
| Past (recent) | na- | *nalonda* — just followed |
| Past (remote) | ka- | *kalonda* — followed (long ago) |
| Future (near) | ta- | *talonda* — will soon follow |
| Future (remote) | ko- | *kolonda* — will follow (distant future) |

**Aspect and mood**

- Perfective (completed): **me-**
- Progressive: **ki-** → *kilonda* — is following
- Habitual / repeated: **sa-** → *salonda* — follows habitually
- Negative: **-te** (suffix) → *londa-te* — does not follow
- Subjunctive / imperative: **tu-** → *tulonda* — let (someone) follow
