**Kintu** is the Universal Language for the Bantusphere. While Swahili has united much of northeastern Africa, a true pan-Bantu bridge is still needed. Strictly Bantu: no Khoisan, Cushitic, Nilotic or West African languages. See [[Kintu/Anthropology]] for the source languages.

## Uniting features

- Noun-class system (consolidated Proto-Bantu classes)
- Prenasalized stops
- Heavily prefixing agglutinative verbs
- Two tones, only high marked
- Five vowels
- Five tenses and three aspects
