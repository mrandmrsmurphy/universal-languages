# Swadesh

> Parent: [[Kintu]] · Cross-UL: [[Swadesh]] · Sounds: [[Kintu/Phonology]] · Script: [[Kintu/Orthography]] · Lexicon: [[Kintu/Lexicon/Lexicon]]

The 207-item Swadesh list with Kintu forms beside its Proto-Bantu source (BLR3) and the six working check languages: Swahili, Kinyarwanda, Lingala, Zulu, Shona, Umbundu (see [[Kintu/Anthropology]]). **—** marks an entry not yet coined; a coined entry links to its lexicon page. Blank comparison cells are unrecorded. Kintu forms are derived from the PB column by the ordered rules in [[Kintu/Phonology#From Proto-Bantu]]; a † marks a plausible extension, not directly attested.

List source: the vault-standard 207 ([[Swadesh]]); Proto-Bantu from BLR3 (Bastin et al. 2002), pronouns from Meeussen's grammatical reconstructions; daughter forms from standard dictionaries, unchecked cells left blank.
