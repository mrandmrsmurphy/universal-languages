## Vowels

| | Front | Back |
| --- | --- | --- |
| High | i | u |
| Mid | e | o |
| Low | a | |

Five vowels, with no height contrast among the mid vowels.

**Stress:** penultimate.

**Tone:** low (unmarked) and high (´). Two tones only, to keep the system simple for speakers of both tonal and non-tonal languages.
