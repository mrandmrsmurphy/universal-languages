Oromo, Amharic, Somali, Tigrinya, and related languages of a long-standing areal zone. The acronym stands for Somalia, Eritrea, Djibouti, Ethiopia and Sudan; Sudan and South Sudan are not distinguished, and Sudan also overlaps with Middle Semitic.

## Uniting features

- Ejectives: pʼ, tʼ, čʼ, kʼ
- Long vs short vowels
- Gemination
- SOV, mostly head-final
- Suffixing cases, including definiteness and focus
- Converbs for subordinate clauses, and light-verb constructions with "say" and "do"
