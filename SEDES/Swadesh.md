# Swadesh

> Parent: [[SEDES]] · Cross-UL: [[Swadesh]] · Sounds: [[SEDES/Phonology]] · Script: [[SEDES/Orthography]]

The 207-item Swadesh list with SEDES forms beside four languages of the Horn of Africa: Amharic, Somali, Oromo and Beja. **—** marks an entry not yet coined, and a coined entry is a link to its lexicon page, which has not been written yet; a blank comparison cell means no form is recorded. Amharic is given in Geʿez script only, since readers of SEDES are expected to know the Geʿez readings; Somali, Oromo and Beja are given in Latin spelling. The Oromo forms are a linguistic transcription, not the standard Qubee spelling.

Sources: the Swadesh lists on Wiktionary, edited for this page (Beja is taken from Wiktionary unchanged). Wiktionary text is available under CC BY-SA.
