# Morphosyntax

> Parent: [[Dravindian]]

Dravindian is SOV, agglutinative, suffixing only, and nominative–accusative. Heads follow their dependents: adjectives and genitives precede the noun, and case and other relations are marked by suffixes or postpositions.

## Nouns

- **Cases:** eight — nominative, accusative, dative, genitive, locative, ablative, instrumental, sociative. The vocative is the bare stem. Each case has one regular ending, with no oblique stems and no sandhi.
- **Gender:** three — rational masculine, rational feminine, and neuter. Gender is semantic, not formal: it shows only in third-person pronouns and verb agreement, never on noun stems.
- **Number:** singular and plural; the plural suffix is -kaḷ 𑀓𑀴𑁆. Number is optional after a numeral.
- **Objects:** the accusative marks animate or definite objects; other objects may be unmarked.
- **Experiencers:** subjects of feeling, liking, wanting and possession take the dative.

## Pronouns

Clusivity: 'we' is inclusive (speaker and addressee) or exclusive (speaker and others). The polite second person is the plural.

| Person | Singular | Plural |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 (plural exclusive) | nān 𑀦𑀸𑀦𑁆 | nāṅ 𑀦𑀸𑀗𑁆 |
| 1 inclusive | — | nām 𑀦𑀸𑀫𑁆 |
| 2 familiar | nī 𑀦𑀻 | nīm 𑀦𑀻𑀫𑁆 |
| 2 polite | nīm 𑀦𑀻𑀫𑁆 | nīm 𑀦𑀻𑀫𑁆 |

Third-person pronouns are built from the demonstrative bases i- (this), a- (that) and e- (which?), with gender and number endings:

| | Masculine | Feminine | Neuter |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| this, sg. | ivan 𑀇𑀯𑀦𑁆 | ivaḷ 𑀇𑀯𑀴𑁆 | idu 𑀇𑀤𑀼 |
| that, sg. | avan 𑀅𑀯𑀦𑁆 | avaḷ 𑀅𑀯𑀴𑁆 | adu 𑀅𑀤𑀼 |
| this, pl. | ivar 𑀇𑀯𑀭𑁆 (rational) | | ivu 𑀇𑀯𑀼 |
| that, pl. | avar 𑀅𑀯𑀭𑁆 (rational) | | avu 𑀅𑀯𑀼 |

The rational plural avar also serves as the honorific singular.

## Case endings

Suffixes follow the stem and any plural -kaḷ. They are identical for nouns and pronouns (nān-ai, nām-ku); there are no oblique stems.

| Case | Ending | Example (maram 'tree') |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nominative | (bare stem) | — |
| Accusative | ai 𑀐 | maram-ai |
| Dative | ku 𑀓𑀼 | maram-ku |
| Genitive | atu 𑀅𑀢𑀼 | maram-atu |
| Locative | il 𑀇𑀮𑁆 | maram-il |
| Ablative | in 𑀇𑀦𑁆 | maram-in |
| Instrumental | āl 𑀆𑀮𑁆 | maram-āl |
| Sociative | ōṭu 𑀑𑀝𑀼 | maram-ōṭu |

Vocative: the bare stem.

**Glide rule.** When a stem or suffix ending in a vowel meets a suffix beginning with a vowel, y is inserted after i, ī, e, ē and v after any other vowel. This is the only joining rule.

## Verbs

Every verb stem ends in a vowel (consonant-final roots take a thematic -u). A finite verb is stem + tense + person ending.

| Tense | Marker |
| --- | --- |
| Past | -t- |
| Non-past | -kk- |
| Future | -v- |
| Negative (no tense) | -ill- |

| Person | Ending |
| --- | --- |
| 1 sg | -ēn |
| 1 pl (exclusive and inclusive) | -ōm |
| 2 sg familiar | -āy |
| 2 pl / polite | -īr |
| 3 sg masc | -ān |
| 3 sg fem | -āḷ |
| 3 sg neuter | -atu |
| 3 pl rational | -ār |
| 3 pl neuter | -ana |

### Other verb forms

| Form | Shape | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Imperative, familiar | bare stem | varu |
| Imperative, polite | stem + -ṅka | varuṅka 𑀯𑀭𑀼𑀗𑁆𑀓 |
| Prohibitive | stem + -ātē | varuvātē 𑀯𑀭𑀼𑀯𑀸𑀢𑁂 |
| Polar question | finite verb + -ā | varutēnā 𑀯𑀭𑀼𑀢𑁂𑀦𑀸 |
| Converb ('having come') | past stem + -u | varutu 𑀯𑀭𑀼𑀢𑀼 |
| Relative participle | tense marker + -a | past varuta 𑀯𑀭𑀼𑀢; non-past varukka 𑀯𑀭𑀼𑀓𑁆𑀓; future varuva 𑀯𑀭𑀼𑀯 |
| Infinitive | stem + -ka | varuka 𑀯𑀭𑀼𑀓 |
| Verbal noun | stem + -tal | varutal 𑀯𑀭𑀼𑀢𑀮𑁆 |
| Conditional | past stem + -āl | varutāl 𑀯𑀭𑀼𑀢𑀸𑀮𑁆 |
| Quotative | converb of en- 'say' | entu 𑁱𑀦𑁆𑀢𑀼 |

- **Chaining:** clauses are joined by converbs, with the finite verb last; the clitic -um joins nouns ('also', 'and').
- **Existential:** uṇṭu 𑀉𑀡𑁆𑀝𑀼 'there is / has' and illa 𑀇𑀮𑁆𑀮 'there is not / has not' are invariable; they do not take person endings.
- **Copula:** none. Predicate nominals take the person endings directly, and the glide rule applies (maruttuvan-ēn 'I am a doctor').
- **Experiencers:** subjects of feeling, liking, wanting and possession take the dative; the verb is then third person.
- **Auxiliaries:** aspect and benefaction by auxiliary verbs after the main verb; causatives are productive.

## Other

- **Questions:** wh-words stay in place (not fronted).
- **Echo words:** reduplication with a changed first syllable gives 'and the like'.
- **Numerals:** decimal, no classifiers.

*Pronoun stems follow the Proto-Dravidian reconstructions, simplified; the first-person plural pair nām/nāṅ is an adaptation of the Tamil–Malayalam pattern. Case endings, tense markers, person endings and non-finite forms are adapted from Tamil, Malayalam, Telugu and Kannada patterns and are not individually verified. Derivational morphology (causative, echo words, nominalizers) has not been coined.*
