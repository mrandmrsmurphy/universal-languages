# Morphosyntax

> Parent: [[Dravindian]]

Dravindian is SOV, agglutinative, and nominative–accusative.

## Settled categories

- **Nouns**: eight cases, three genders
- **Pronouns**: clusivity (inclusive vs. exclusive 'we'); T–V distinction in the second person
- **Verbs**: carry negation and the polar-question marker

*Forms for these categories have not yet been coined — no case endings, pronoun paradigm, or verb morphology exists yet.*
