A Universal Language for the Dravidian languages of southeast India — Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Tulu, Gondi, and Proto-Dravidian itself. Sister project to [[Neo-Sanskrit]] for the subcontinent's other major family.

## Uniting features

- Eight cases, three genders
- Inclusive/exclusive distinction (clusivity)
- Agglutinative morphology
- Five vowels, each short and long
- Gemination of any consonant
- T–V distinction (familiar vs. polite 'you')
- Negation and polar questions marked on the verb
- SOV word order
- Native stops contrast voiced and unvoiced only — no aspirated or creaky series
