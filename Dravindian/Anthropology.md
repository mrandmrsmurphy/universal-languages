# Anthropology

> Parent: [[Dravindian]]

![[Drivindian_flag.svg\|180]]

Dravindian is a Universal Language for the Dravidian languages of southeast India. Its sources are Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Tulu, Gondi, and reconstruction from Proto-Dravidian. It is the sister project to [[Neo-Sanskrit]], covering the subcontinent's other major family.

## Family features

The design rests on the family's shared traits:

- **Eight cases**
- **Three genders**
- **Clusivity** — inclusive vs. exclusive 'we'
- **Agglutinative morphology**
- **Five vowels**, short and long
- **Gemination** of consonants
- **T–V distinction** in the second person
- **Negation marked on verbs**
- **Polar questions marked on the verb**
- **SOV** word order
- **Stops contrast voiced and unvoiced only** in native words — no aspirated or creaky series
