# Phonology

> Parent: [[Balkan]]

By allowing individual differences of realization, Balkan keeps as coherent a set of sounds as possible across the region.

The affricates and sibilants each cover an alveolar-to-palatal realization range (ts~tʃ~tɕ, dz~dʒ~dʑ, s~ʃ, z~ʒ); speakers use their local variant. /θ/ is marginal, appearing only in the transcription of foreign words. The sequences /ks/ and /ps/ are common enough to have their own letters.

## Vowels

| | Front | Mid | Back |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| High | i | | u |
| Mid | e | ə | o~ɔ |

Six vowels: the region's typical five-vowel system plus schwa, the product of vowel reduction in unstressed syllables.

## Prosody

Stress is predictable and right-defined (counted from the right edge of the word). The name itself is penult-stressed: Бαλκαν 'bal.kan.
