# Anthropology

> Parent: [[Balkan]]

![[Balkan_flag.png\|180]]

Balkan is a Universal Language, a regional auxiliary language meant to codify the [Balkan sprachbund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balkan_sprachbund). Despite being very distant languages from each other, the members of the sprachbund have formed a shared heritage over the centuries.

## Member languages

- Greek
- (Ottoman) Turkish
- Albanian
- Serbian and the other South Slavic languages of the peninsula (absorbed from the [[Interslavic]] split)
- Romani, Aromanian and Gagauz
- Other members of the Balkan sprachbund; Romanian is shared with [[Interlingua]]

## Sprachbund features

The design codifies the classic convergent traits of the zone:

- **Vowel reduction in unstressed syllables**
- **Loss of Slavic pitch accent**
- **Reduced case** — prepositions and word order carry grammatical relations
- **Postposed definite article**
- **Periphrastic future tense** built on the verb 'want', reduced to the particle *da*; no synthetic future
- **Infinitives replaced by subordinate clauses**
- **Analytic comparative construction**
- **Genitive–dative merger** — one oblique case serves for both
- **Perfect built with 'have'**
- **Evidentiality** — a small system marking reported or inferred information
- **Teens built 'one-on-ten'** (11 = one-on-ten)
- **Dative clitic doubling**
- **Strong Ottoman Turkish influence on vocabulary**
